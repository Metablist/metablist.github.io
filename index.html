About Me

I am a Fabric mod developer and graphic designer, known in-game as MouseTweak. Coding is purely a hobby for me; I build projects in my free time and have no plans to monetize them right now. I focus on creating unique, performance-optimized, and immersive Minecraft modifications.

Outside of game modification, my primary hobbies include graphic design and Minecraft PvP. I also possess exceptional cyber security knowledge and specialize in screensharing and thoroughly PC-checking players for cheats and illicit modifications.
